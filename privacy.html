Privacy Policy

Effective 16 November 2026

We respect your privacy. This policy explains what we collect when you use zelqio.in and how we use it.

What we collect

  • Your Google account name and email address when you sign in.
  • Your phone number, delivery address and the items you order.
  • Payment status and payment ID from Razorpay. We do not receive your card, UPI or bank details.
  • Basic technical information such as browser type, needed to run the site.

Why we use it

To process and deliver your order, send receipts and updates, give support, prevent fraud and meet legal and tax requirements.

Who we share it with

  • Razorpay, to take your payment.
  • Our printing and courier partners, only the name, address and phone number needed to make and deliver your order.
  • Google, for sign-in.
  • Authorities, where the law requires.

We do not sell your personal data.

Storage and security

Order records are kept in a protected business account with access limited to the owner. We keep them for as long as needed for orders, support and legal or tax requirements.

Cookies

We do not use advertising or tracking cookies. Google sign-in and Razorpay may set their own cookies as part of their services.

Your choices

You can ask us to show, correct or delete your personal data by contacting us, subject to what we must keep by law.

Children

This site is not meant for purchases by anyone under 18.

Changes

We may update this policy; the latest version is always on this page.
