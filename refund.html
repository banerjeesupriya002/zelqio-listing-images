Refund and Cancellation Policy (No Returns)

Effective 16 November 2026
No returns. No exchanges. Every ZELQIO product is made to order for you, so we cannot accept returns or exchanges for change of mind, size, colour or design preference.

Damaged, defective or wrong item

If your item arrives damaged, defective or is not what you ordered, contact us within 48 hours of delivery with your order ID, clear photos and, if possible, an unboxing video. If we verify the issue, we will send a replacement or refund you, at our option.

How refunds are paid

Approved refunds go back to the original payment method within 5 to 7 business days after approval, depending on your bank or payment provider.

Cancellation

Production begins soon after payment, so orders cannot be cancelled once production has started. If you need to cancel, contact us immediately. If your order has not gone into production, we will cancel it and refund you.

Failed or double payments

If money was deducted but your order was not confirmed, or you were charged twice, it is normally refunded automatically within 5 to 7 business days. If not, contact us with your payment ID.
